RULES

How to add feedback

Every feedback entry gets its own HTML page under feedback/days/. Use feedback/TEMPLATE.html, replace the placeholders, then commit the new page.

Keep one entry focused on one publishing result or learning event. Record what was published, the source story, angle, platform, format, results, your interpretation, what worked, what failed, and what Content-OS should learn.

Use YYYY-MM-DD-short-description.html for filenames. Never overwrite historical entries unless correcting an explicit error.

After creating the page, add its metadata to feedback/archive.js. This automatically makes it appear in this index.
