RULES

How to add feedback

Every feedback entry gets its own HTML page under feedback/days/. Use the template at feedback/TEMPLATE.html, replace the placeholders, then commit the new page.

Keep one entry focused on one publishing result or learning event. Record the content, the angle, the format, the result, your judgment, what you think caused the result, and what Content-OS should change or preserve.

Use a date-based filename: YYYY-MM-DD-short-description.html. Do not overwrite old feedback entries. The archive is the historical record.

After adding a page, add the same entry to feedback/archive.js so it appears on this index.
